Kiln Ticket

Privacy

Kiln Ticket stores customer names, contact details, photographs and firing records on your device. The developer does not receive these records. The app contains no advertising or analytics tools.

Photos and messages

The app uses the camera only when you choose to take a picture. You can also select a photo with the system photo picker. You choose the destination for every pickup message and exported backup.

Purchases and storage

Apple processes purchases and supplies purchase status to the app. Apple device backups may include studio records, depending on your device settings. Exported files remain wherever you save them. To remove local studio data, delete the app. Delete exported backups separately.

Questions

Contact Daria Kopaliani through support. Please omit private studio records.
